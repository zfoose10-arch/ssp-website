# SIGNAL INTEGRITY
## A method for finding where truth dies in an organization, deciding whether that is what is costing it, and repairing the one circuit that matters

Version 4, minted 2026-10-05. The canonical consulting method of Summit Spring Partners. Built from nine sources and nothing else: Meadows, Thinking in Systems; Meadows, Randers and Meadows, Limits to Growth; Senge, The Fifth Discipline; Stroh, Systems Thinking for Social Change; Schein, Organizational Culture and Leadership; Schein, Humble Consulting; Block, Flawless Consulting; Robson, The Intelligence Trap; Snowden, Cynefin; Jackson, Critical Systems Thinking. Eleven elements from the prior SSP framework were folded in after passing a single bar: each closes a failure the method would otherwise have in a real room. Nothing was folded in for coverage.*

\* The prior SSP framework (Master Practitioner Guide, Engagement Process, Field Card, Stage 0 RTCOS, the twelve pattern library, the control map, and their supplements) is archived, not deleted. In a live engagement, a practitioner may consult it when a situation at hand maps directly onto something it covers in more detail, such as a specific pattern's diagnostic test or a verbatim phrase. It is reference, never doctrine. Nothing from it is added to this method except through the nugget bar above, and nothing in it overrides what is written here.


## 1. The Thesis

An organization is an information processing system that runs on humans. Reality generates signals constantly, from customers, from the market, from the front line, from the numbers. The organization survives to the degree that those signals reach someone who can act, arrive undistorted, and convert into a decision before the delay makes them irrelevant.

The costliest problems an organization carries are rarely knowledge problems. Somewhere in the building, someone already knows. The problems persist because the signal dies before it converts into action. It dies in a culture where the messenger pays a price. It dies against the certainty of the smartest person in the room, whose intelligence makes them better at defending a wrong position rather than abandoning it. It dies in unspoken payoffs the people in charge collect from the status quo. It dies in delays long enough that when the metric finally moves, the damage is already structural. It dies in reward systems that quietly pay people to look away. And it dies in the filtering layer, middle management, where every day people decide what travels upward and in what form.

The method asks one question and everything else serves it:

> Where does truth die here, is that death the binding constraint on the thing that is costing us, and what is it costing?

The middle clause is the discipline. Signal failure is present in every organization the way findings are present on every full body scan. The method earns its keep by deciding whether the failure is the constraint that binds, or merely present beside a different constraint. When it is merely present, the method says so and declines to sell the repair.

## 2. The Epistemic Stance

The method does not sell verdicts. Complex systems defeat advance analysis; cause and effect are knowable only in retrospect, and the leverage points that matter are counterintuitive and usually pushed in the wrong direction by people certain they understand the system. A method built on that knowledge cannot promise to name the single decisive cause from a few weeks of interviews.

So the diagnostic produces a First Probe Target: the practitioner's strongest hypothesis about where the binding break lies, stated with explicit confidence, presented beside its strongest competing explanation, argued honestly. The probes that follow are the test of that hypothesis, not the execution of a verdict. The real load bearing break is something the engagement discovers through probing, and the method says that out loud, to the client, before any money changes hands.

Every diagnosis passes an external peer check before it reaches the client: one standing reviewer, paid specifically to argue the competing diagnosis, disclosed to the client. The method requires disconfirmation structures of its clients and imposes the same on itself, because the practitioner collects a payoff from elegant singular findings and cannot be the only check on that bias. Intelligence and seniority amplify motivated reasoning; they do not protect against it. That applies to the person holding the map first.

## 3. The Intellectual Architecture

Each source carries one load bearing contribution, and the leverage comes from the joints between them.

Meadows, Thinking in Systems, supplies the leverage hierarchy. Interventions at parameters are weak; interventions at information flows, rules, goals, and paradigms are strong. Stocks, flows, and delays are the grammar of diagnosis. The draining stock nobody measures, and the delay that hides the drain, are where chronic problems live.

Limits to Growth supplies overshoot dynamics. Growth plus delayed feedback produces collapse, and an organization outruns its human carrying capacity, trust, management bandwidth, cultural coherence, years before the financials show it.

Senge supplies the archetype library as a pattern recognition instrument, Shifting the Burden, Fixes That Fail, Limits to Growth, Eroding Goals, and the discipline of mental models as the hidden layer under every decision. Dialogue, inquiry balanced against advocacy, is the medium in which repairs get made.

Stroh supplies the change spine: build a foundation, face current reality, make an explicit choice, bridge the gap. He also supplies the two findings most firms refuse to touch: the system is producing exactly what its most powerful actors are, in some way, paid to keep producing, and people only act on analysis they helped build.

Schein, Organizational Culture and Leadership, supplies the three layers, artifacts, espoused values, basic assumptions, and the finding that truth dies at the assumption layer. He also supplies the embedding mechanisms: culture is installed and changed through what leaders attend to, measure, reward, and model, how they react in a crisis, where they send resources, and above all whom they select, promote, and remove. Repairs that are not installed through those levers do not survive.

Schein, Humble Consulting, supplies the relationship as instrument: help from the first conversation, earn trust incrementally rather than demand it contractually, prefer adaptive moves to grand solutions, and treat the here and now in the room as the most reliable data available.

Block supplies contracting as the pivotal act, wants and offers stated on both sides, authentic behavior as technique, resistance named in the room rather than managed around it, and the fifty fifty responsibility split. A recommendation delivered to a client becomes a shelf item; a finding reached with a client becomes a commitment.

Robson supplies the intelligence trap: motivated reasoning scales with intelligence, expertise produces earned dogmatism, and the remedies are intellectual humility, actively open minded thinking, considering the opposite, and the premortem, applied to client and practitioner alike.

Snowden supplies domain diagnosis before method selection. Clear problems have best practice, complicated problems yield to expert analysis and good practice, complex problems require probe, sense, respond with safe to fail experiments. Treating a complex problem as complicated is the signature failure of expert consulting. Narrative sensemaking lets the front line be heard without the filtering layer in the way.

Jackson supplies methodological pluralism as a discipline rather than a convenience, and boundary critique: ask who has been excluded from the conversation, because the missing voice usually carries the dying signal, and ask it of the method itself. He also supplies the field read: a room is unitary when its people share goals, pluralist when they hold compatible interests but different worldviews, and coercive when interests cannot be reconciled by discussion and apparent agreement is compliance under power. The read decides what kind of truth session is even possible.

The joints: Snowden decides which tools apply. Meadows decides where to aim them. Schein and Robson explain why the signal dies. Stroh and Block govern how the humans come to own the repair. Jackson polices the boundaries and blind spots of everyone involved, including the practitioner.

## 4. The Five Instruments

These are the method's tools. The movements in section 5 are where they are used.

### 4.1 The Domain Read

Before any tool leaves the bag: is this problem clear, complicated, or complex? The tell for complex is usually in plain sight: the client already hired experts and it did not work, or the same fix has been applied three times with the same result. The tell for complicated is the opposite: nobody with the right expertise has looked yet, and when they do the cause will be findable. The domain read decides whether the engagement runs probes or runs analysis, and it is wrong to decide that from inside either habit.

The failed fix history is read with care, because it is the most abused evidence in the trade. Every prior fix is given one of three readings: it addressed the wrong layer, so the layer is not where the cause lives; it addressed the right layer but a competing rule, usually a reward or a decision right, fought it back, so the competing rule is the target; or it was correct and abandoned inside its own delay, in which case it is unfinished, not failed, and counts as evidence of nothing. Only the first two readings are failures. A client whose fixes were all the third kind may have a complicated problem and a patience problem, not a complex one.

### 4.2 The Signal Autopsy

Take the costly problem and trace it backward through the people. Who knew first? When? What did they do with the knowing? Where did it stop? What happened to the last person who pushed it? This runs through ten to fifteen conversations plus artifact review, hearing raw narrative before interpretation, and it has two required stops that are easy to skip. The departed dissenter: every organization has someone who pushed this signal and paid for it, sidelined, demoted, or gone, and their account is the least filtered one available, so they are interviewed, including alumni. The filtering layer: middle managers are interviewed not as witnesses but as the population whose daily choices constitute the signal path.

After every conversation, three things are written down before the next one begins: the strongest concrete artifact the person described, the gap between what they say they believe and what their story shows, and the assumption forming, stated specifically rather than as trust issues or culture problems. The First Probe Target's confidence level is only as defensible as these notes.

Two questions are asked early, because their answers change the engagement's shape. The field read: is this room unitary, pluralist, or coercive? Coercive signals, a board or investor constraint, a protected person, careful language around one name, mean the Mirror Sequence cannot be run as a consensus exercise and the engagement is bounded honestly now rather than discovered to be coerced three weeks in. And in a founder led company, the founder question, asked directly: do you want to scale beyond what your personal capacity can carry, or keep the company at the size where your capacity is the system? Both are valid and lead to different companies. A founder who cannot answer has a company in suspension, and no repair installs until the decision is made. The method designs around the answer the founder gives, never the one the practitioner prefers.

### 4.3 The Fuel and Hands Test

Signal problems, capacity problems, and capability problems all present identically in interviews: everyone says "we all knew." The test separates them by following the last three attempts to act on the truth. When it reached someone who could act, did action fail for fear, for fuel, or for hands?

Fear looks like softened messages, delayed meetings, and reframing before presentation. The signal is being killed, and this method is the right instrument.

Fuel looks like honest prioritization calls, started and abandoned workstreams, and open acknowledgment with no movement. The signal arrived; the organization lacks the bandwidth, capital, or talent depth to act on it. The binding constraint is capacity, and a signal repair will produce a beautifully clear picture of a problem the client still cannot staff.

Hands looks like the signal arriving at the right seat, the person in that seat trying in good faith, and the attempt failing because the person cannot do what the seat requires. The binding constraint is capability, individual or team, and the honest finding is a capability finding, not a culture finding. This lane exists because it is the one practitioners most want to avoid naming, and because misdiagnosing a capability gap as a signal failure sends a leadership team to fix its culture when it needs to fix a hire.

"Present but not binding" is a sanctioned finding. When fuel or hands bind, the finding is delivered as such, the engagement reframes toward the true constraint or declines the repair, and the practice says so out loud. The refusal, exercised and mentioned, is worth more than the fee.

### 4.4 The Stakes Ledger

A number the practitioner models is dismantled by a competent finance leader in one meeting. A number the finance leader builds cannot be dismantled by the finance leader, because they built it. The Stakes Ledger is a sixty to ninety minute working session with the chief executive and the finance lead in which the practitioner asks questions and writes down only what they say.

Three rules govern it. The client holds the pen; every figure was spoken aloud before it was written. Past cost only, never future savings; the ledger counts what the problem has already cost, documented and past tense, because projected benefit is a forecast and forecasts are negotiable, while documented cost is a fact and facts compound. Two columns, and only one is ever quoted; Conservative holds only what the finance lead would defend to a board without preparation, Expected holds their honest middle read, and every fee conversation and every claim the practice ever makes uses the Conservative column alone.

Four lanes carry the cost, ordered from most documented to most estimated so the room builds confidence on hard numbers before reaching judgment calls. Direct recurring cost: rework, credits, churn, expedited spend, already in the ledger. Turnover and absorption: regretted departures where the problem was a factor, plus misses absorbed rather than addressed. The stalled initiative: the named thing that should have shipped and has not, the margin it was built to carry times the months it has sat; usually the largest number and the one the chief executive actually cares about. Decision delay and leadership drain: cycle time on the affected decision class times frequency, plus leadership hours consumed; real but soft, so include it and let it be discounted heavily, which buys credibility for the rest.

The session closes with the board test: read the Conservative column back and ask whether they would defend it as written tomorrow. Lower any line until the hesitation disappears, at their direction. Then get the sentence out loud: this costs at least this much a year, and that is your number, not mine. A thin ledger is not a facilitation failure; it is evidence that the problem is present but not binding, and it routes back to the Fuel and Hands Test rather than to creative counting.

The Conservative column is the Scoreboard from the day it is built. It is the number the engagement is judged against, by the client, on the client's own accounting. Every probe in the Repair carries two criteria written in its terms: a leading one, that the signal now arrives where it used to die, and a lagging one, that a named ledger lane has moved. Both durability checks re-read the same four lanes with the finance lead holding the pen again. The method never scores itself; it hands the client the number they built and asks them to read it.

### 4.5 The Reward Audit

Among the strong leverage points, rules, including incentives and punishments, sit just above information flows, and in most organizations the compensation and promotion system is the literal broken circuit. What really gets rewarded, not what is espoused, published, or preached, teaches every person what to carry upward and what to bury. A product manager rewarded for selling a plan to senior management rather than for the plan's results learns to build plans that please senior management. A sales force paid on bookings learns to book what will not ship. A plant paid on output learns to run the line past the point where quality signals should have stopped it.

The audit asks three questions of every role on the signal path. What does this role actually get paid, promoted, and praised for, in practice, over the last two years? What signal would a rational person in this role suppress to protect that reward? Where on the path did the autopsy find the signal dying, and does that location match a role whose reward depends on it not arriving? When the dying signal and a reward structure line up, the repair target is the rule, not the person, and no amount of candor training will outlast a pay plan that punishes candor.

## 5. The Method: Five Movements

### Movement 0: Entry

Two conversations, no pitch. In the first, the practitioner helps visibly with something real; the client should feel genuinely helped before anything is sold. In the second, the practitioner contracts explicitly: what the client wants, what the practitioner wants, what each offers and refuses, and honest notice that the work will eventually examine the leadership team itself, with the deeper permission for that to be asked when it has been earned rather than demanded now. The field read begins here, from how the chief executive talks about the board, the investors, and the people whose names they handle carefully.

Three gates. The chief executive contracts personally rather than delegating, because their own attention, payoffs, and certainties are inside the system under examination. The anchor problem is nameable and plausibly costly. The chief executive consents to the trace beginning. Full examination permission is not a gate; it is a product of the first two movements, requested at the moment the method predicts it becomes grantable. Demanding it on day one selects for leaders who least need the method and screens out exactly the leaders whose certainties are the pathology.

### Movement 1: The Circuit Trace

The Domain Read first. If the problem is complicated, the engagement exits to the Complicated Lane in section 6 and does not continue here. If complex, the trace runs as discovery with the client rather than inspection of them, interleaved with the first two Mirror sessions so that trust and truth accumulate together.

The Signal Autopsy, with the departed dissenter and the filtering layer as required conversations, the field read confirmed, and the founder question asked where it applies. The layer check: what the artifacts claim, what the values espouse, what the assumptions actually run. The loop and stock map: one causal loop, one named archetype, the draining stock nobody measures, and the delay concealing the drain. The boundary critique: whose voice is structurally missing from the decisions where the missing signal lives. The payoff surfacing: what the current design quietly delivers to the people with the power to change it. The trap audit: where somebody's certainty is functioning as the blockage. The Reward Audit along the signal path. The Fuel and Hands Test on the last three attempts to act. The Stakes Ledger session with the finance lead, run once the problem has a preliminary name.

When the payoff turns out to be a person, someone the chief executive is protecting, the trace reads the chief executive's posture before anything else is decided. The chief executive who knows and is choosing the protection engages directly, can name the cost, and has decided it is acceptable; that protection is designed around, not surfaced, and the engagement's ceiling is stated honestly. The chief executive who knows but has not faced it hedges, describes incidents but resists the pattern, and has postponed the decision quarter after quarter; that protection is surfaced, with rigorous cost data, as a deferred decision made visible. The chief executive who genuinely does not see it is surprised by specifics, because the protected person performs differently for them or because the team cannot say it; that is a filtering failure first, and naming the protection before the information can travel produces a leader who feels ambushed. The rule: name the protection only in the second posture and only with the data in hand, because naming is a one way door and rejection at that point is a relationship break, not a disagreement.

The output is a one page Signal Map and the First Probe Target, with its strongest competitor beside it and confidence stated. Before anything reaches the client, the draft passes the external peer check.

### Movement 2: The Mirror Sequence

Three sessions, graduated, because a single confrontation day violates the method's own library: foundations take time, confrontation before trust produces defensiveness rather than learning, and resistance surfaces late and emotionally. Each session is a rehearsal for the next, so the team twice practices facing smaller truths and surviving it before the choice arrives. In a coercive field the sequence is bounded rather than skipped: the truths that can be faced are faced, the constraint is named as a constraint, and no consensus is manufactured where the room cannot afford one.

Session One, ninety minutes, a small truth. One early, low threat finding from the trace, walked together. The team experiences honest examination without injury, and resistance that surfaces is welcomed and worked rather than managed.

Session Two, half a day, the system. The draft Signal Map and causal structure, including the filtering layer's view, the Reward Audit's findings, and the competing diagnosis argued honestly. The team corrects, contests, and thereby co-authors the map. Pushback is sorted as it arrives. Pushback on language is incorporated. Pushback on substance is worked through with evidence. Defense, a flash of heat, denial, deflection, is anxiety about what the finding means, and it is answered with safety and tentative framing, never with more proof. The loudest flag is the opposite of pushback: easy agreement on the finding expected to be hardest means the team has not engaged it, and it is surfaced before anything moves forward. At the close, with the map now partly theirs, the practitioner asks for full permission: to bring the leadership team's own payoffs and certainties into the final session.

Session Three, one day, the payoff and the choice. Current reality faced in full: this outcome is what the design reliably produces, its designers are in the room, and here is what the design pays them. The Stakes Ledger's Conservative number is on the wall. A premortem is run on the proposed repair. Then the explicit choice: keep the payoff and accept the cost knowingly, or trade the payoff for the repair. Either honest answer is respected. A polite yes ends the engagement here, cleanly.

This is the Fork, and it is a named moment rather than an outcome, because the client bought a diagnosis scoped to their presenting problem and the diagnosis is complete whichever way they turn. Three exits are sanctioned and each is a good engagement. Commission the repair. Keep the payoff knowingly and stop, with the diagnosis in hand. Or reframe, because the Fuel and Hands Test showed that capacity or capability binds, toward the true constraint or toward the Complicated Lane. Nothing about the repair is paid for or begun until the client has turned, and the practice prices the diagnostic arc so that a client who stops here was a profitable engagement rather than a failed sale.

The filtering layer attends Session Two and the repair design portion of Session Three. The people whose behavior carries the signal do not learn about the repair secondhand.

### Movement 3: The Repair

No rollout, no program office. Two or three safe to fail probes run in parallel, testing the First Probe Target: small, cheap, contradictory by design, with amplification and shutdown criteria written before launch. Probes are aimed at the highest available leverage: usually a new information flow that makes an invisible cost visible to the people who create it, or a changed rule about who decides, who must be heard, and what gets rewarded; occasionally the goal itself. Never a parameter tweak, never a reorg line. When the Reward Audit found the rule, the rule is the first probe. Every probe ships with one primary lever commitment from the chief executive, drawn from the six: what they will now attend to and measure, how they will react in the next crisis, where resources go, what they will model, what gets rewarded, or whom they will select, promote, or remove. A probe without a lever commitment is not designed, because machinery has no authority without supply from the leader.

Probe teams are constituted from the filtering layer with real design authority. The practitioner coaches the team's own sensemaking on a light cadence rather than operating the probes. The sensing reviews are where the real break reveals itself: probes that move the cost confirm the hypothesis, probes that do not are honest evidence for the competitor, and the method redirects without embarrassment because that was the stated bargain from Entry.

Each review classifies every probe in one of four words: holding, fragile, regressing, or stalled, and ambiguity defaults to the lower word. No verdict is given inside a probe's stated delay, because a correct move judged before its effect can arrive is unfinished, not failing, and checking faster than the system responds measures the practitioner's presence rather than the move. When a probe stalls, the question is which anxiety binds. If the cost is not felt, sharpen the disconfirmation. If the cost is felt but the new behavior makes people feel exposed and incompetent, add safety, rehearsal, coaching, never pressure. And the default is to hold rather than adjust: a system pulling back toward its old goal is a balancing loop defending itself, which means the probe found the right link and needs reinforcement, while a pattern simply continuing unchanged means the probe never interrupted the loop that sustains it, which is the only case that earns an adjustment.

### Movement 4: Embed and Exit

The proven repair is installed through the embedding levers, what leadership now attends to, measures, rewards, and models, how they react in crisis, where they send resources, and whom they select and remove, so it no longer depends on anyone's goodwill. Before exit is allowed, the chief executive claims the repair as their own, in front of the team, in their own language, not in a speech but in small moments under pressure: correcting a reversion in real time, declining to revert when it would be easy, referencing the new pattern in a performance conversation. A team watches to learn whether this was the consultant's initiative or the leader's expectation, and an announcement without that behavior tells them it was the consultant's. The client team is taught the circuit trace and runs one on a second, smaller problem with the practitioner observing. Formal exit.

Two durability checks follow: at ninety days, for early reversion signals, and at month nine, which is the only one that counts. Culture reverts on six to eighteen month cycles, and the method will not certify success inside the reversion delay it teaches. Only the month nine result appears in any published claim. Both checks re-read the Scoreboard's four lanes with the finance lead holding the pen.

## 6. The Complicated Lane

Clients do not arrive pre-sorted, and some problems that look like signal failures are complicated: a cause exists, nobody with the right expertise has looked, and when they do it will be findable. The Domain Read catches these, and sending them through probes would be malpractice in the other direction.

The Complicated Lane is a short, fixed scope analytical engagement. The practitioner either brings the expertise or names who does, runs the analysis, delivers the finding with a recommended good practice fix, and stops. It still uses the Stakes Ledger to size the problem and the Fuel and Hands Test to confirm the organization can act on the answer, because a correct analysis handed to a team with no fuel or no hands is a shelf item. It does not run the Mirror, because there is no payoff to surface when the cause was simply unknown. It exists so that adjacent revenue from people who already trust the practice has somewhere to land, and so that the method never forces a complex tool onto a complicated problem to keep a client in the pipeline.

## 7. The Execution Seam

The method is a diagnosis and choice engine that is deliberately thin on execution, and most buyers want someone to do the work with them. The honest answer is a designed handoff rather than a bench. The practice names two or three partner categories, operators and implementation firms, defines the seam precisely, where the method's work ends and theirs begins, and stays attached through both durability checks so the handoff does not become an exit. The seam matters more than the partners: a buyer who hears exactly who executes and that the practitioner remains accountable through month nine does not need the practitioner to execute.

---

## 8. Delivery and Pricing

The buyer is the chief executive, contracting personally. Sponsors and boards may open the door and fund the work; the operating chief executive owns the contract or the engagement is declined.

Delivery is principal led, one practitioner, with at most one associate for logistics and mapping, plus the external peer reviewer retained per engagement. Trust does not delegate, and scale comes from method density rather than headcount.

Pricing is fixed fee per movement, never hourly, anchored to the Stakes Ledger's Conservative column rather than to modeled future savings. The diagnostic arc, Movements 1 and 2, is priced to sustain the practice as a standalone product, because its structure invites the client to stop at the Fork and a client who does so was a profitable engagement rather than a failed conversion. A credit from the diagnostic fee applies toward the repair when commissioned within sixty days of the Fork. The Complicated Lane is priced as a small fixed fee product. Durability checks are included. A standing retainer for quarterly circuit reviews is available after exit, capacity permitting.

## 9. The Standing Disciplines

These are the rules the method imposes on itself. They are not optional and the practitioner may not grant themselves exceptions.

Hypothesis, never verdict: First Probe Target language in every document, every session, every sales conversation.

The peer check on every Signal Map, disclosed to the client, paid to disagree.

The Fuel and Hands Test in every trace, with "present but not binding" as a sanctioned finding and a practiced walk away path.

The departed dissenter interview, every engagement.

The filtering layer inside the Mirror and the probes.

The Stakes Ledger built with the finance lead holding the pen, Conservative column only, past cost only, and read back as the Scoreboard at both durability checks.

The Reward Audit along the signal path before any repair is designed, because a repair installed over a pay plan that punishes it will not survive the pay plan.

The Domain Read before any tool leaves the bag, with the Complicated Lane as the honest exit.

Permission earned, never gated; the deep ask comes at Session Two.

The Fork named and honored: three sanctioned exits, nothing about the repair paid for or begun until the client has turned.

A probe is never judged inside its own delay, and a stalled probe gets safety before it gets pressure.

The protection of a person is named only in the second posture, with the data in hand, because naming is a one way door.

Month nine is the scoreboard; no published claim rests on day ninety.

The refusal, exercised and mentioned; the practice declines repairs it cannot honestly recommend and tells the story of having done so.
